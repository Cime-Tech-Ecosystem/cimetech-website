Privacy Policy.

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Who We Are

Cime Tech Ecosystem Ltd. ("Cime", "we", "us") runs The Cime Hub, a co-working space and community, and provides technology services including software development, network and infrastructure setup, cloud migration, cybersecurity governance and business intelligence. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

Information We Collect

When you use our Book a Hub Tour or Start a Project forms, or contact us directly, we collect:

  • ▲Your name, phone number and email address
  • ▲The details of your enquiry, such as the space or service you are interested in, your preferred date and time, the number of people and any project details you choose to share

If analytics is switched on, we also collect basic, aggregated usage information such as pages viewed and button clicks.

How We Collect It

Our forms prepare a message with your details that opens in WhatsApp, and you press Send to submit it. You can also call, email or message us directly. We only receive what you choose to send.

Why We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange a Hub tour, scope and deliver a project, and keep in touch about your request. We rely on your consent, which you give when you submit a form or contact us, and on our legitimate interest in running and improving our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Who We Share It With

We share information only with people and providers who help us respond to you or run our website, such as our messaging, email and hosting providers, and only as far as needed. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to deal with your request and our related business records, and then delete or anonymise them.

Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • ▲Be informed about how your data is used
  • ▲Access the personal data we hold about you
  • ▲Have inaccurate data corrected
  • ▲Ask us to delete your data or restrict how we use it
  • ▲Object to processing, including direct marketing
  • ▲Receive your data in a portable format
  • ▲Withdraw your consent at any time

To use any of these rights, email hello@cimetechsystem.com. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

How We Protect It

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, and limit access to the people who need it to respond to you.

Analytics and Cookies

This website may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages and buttons are used. This helps us improve the site. It is not used to identify you personally.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
